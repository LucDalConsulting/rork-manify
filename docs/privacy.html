Privacy Policy

Manify · Last updated June 16, 2026

Manify ("Manify," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the Manify mobile application (the "App") handles and how. By using the App you agree to this policy.

Information We Collect

  • Account & profile: The App does not require you to create an account or sign in to use it. Your lesson progress, streaks, bookmarks, and preferences are stored locally on your device.
  • Purchases: In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to validate purchases and unlock your membership. RevenueCat processes your purchase history, a randomly generated app-user identifier, and basic device/OS information to deliver your entitlement. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy and Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Notifications: If you enable reminders, the App uses Apple Push Notification service to deliver them. An anonymous push token may be processed for this purpose. You can turn notifications off at any time in iOS Settings.
  • No advertising or third-party analytics SDKs are included in the App, and we do not use advertising identifiers (IDFA).

How We Use Information

  • To provide and operate the App and your lesson experience.
  • To deliver and restore your in-app purchase / membership.
  • To send reminders that you have opted into.

Data Storage & Retention

Your progress and preferences are stored on your device. Deleting the App removes this local data. Purchase records are retained by Apple and RevenueCat as needed to provide and restore your membership.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with the service providers needed to run the App — Apple (payments, notifications) and RevenueCat (purchase validation).

Children

The App is intended for a general/teen-and-older audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your Choices & Rights

Because most data is stored locally, uninstalling the App deletes it. To request deletion of purchase-related data or to ask a privacy question, contact us at the email below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
