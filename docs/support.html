Frequently Asked Questions

How does the membership work?

Manify Membership is a one-time purchase that unlocks lifetime access to all tiers and courses — no subscription, no recurring charges.

I bought the membership on another device / reinstalled. How do I get it back?

Open the app, go to the upgrade screen, and tap Restore Purchases. Your membership is tied to your Apple ID and will be restored for free.

I was charged but the app didn't unlock.

Tap Restore Purchases first. If it still doesn't unlock, email us with your purchase receipt and we'll sort it out right away.

How do I turn lesson reminders on or off?

Reminders are controlled in your iPhone's Settings → Notifications → Manify.

How do I request a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Refund requests go through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
